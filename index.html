Chapter 02

A 25-Year Mountain Legacy Born in the Himalayas

From Teahouse Trails to Global Horizons

In Nepal, the trails are not just routes on a map; they are living traditions. For over 25 years, my father has guided hikers, mountaineers, and seekers through the Annapurna sanctuaries, Everest high passes, and remote sacred villages.

I spent my upbringing absorbing his deep mountain wisdom, patience, and the unconditional warmth of Himalayan hospitality. When I began traveling through the American alpine wilderness, the Pacific Northwest coastlines, and historic European alleys, that foundational philosophy came with me.

Now, I help travelers experience the world through that same intimate lens—combining safety, local access, and genuine aesthetic wonder.
