Chapter 02

From Balance Sheets to Big Data — Driven by a Lifelong Wanderlust

Analytical Mind, Adventurer's Spirit

My academic path began in Accounting, where I developed precision, discipline, and an appreciation for structured thinking. Today, as a graduate student in Data Science, I immerse myself in modeling complex patterns, data architecture, and predictive analytics.

But long before lines of code and financial statements, a deep instinct pulled me outdoors. Raised with profound respect for the Himalayas—inspired by my family’s 25-year mountain guiding heritage in Nepal—adventure has never been just a hobby for me. It is my compass and creative oxygen.

Whenever I close my laptop, I head for the wild: from scrambling boulder fields to glacial lakes in the Grand Tetons, watching the sunrise over Utah's red arches, to wandering historic canals in Brugge and coastal cliffs on the French and Italian Rivieras. This space is my visual journal celebrating everywhere I have walked.
