Chapter 02

Nepal Roots, Data Science, and an Endless Need to Wander

Where It All Started

If you ask me why I’m so obsessed with traveling and hiking, the answer is simple: I grew up in Nepal. When your childhood backdrop is the Himalayas, mountains aren’t just a pretty sight on a postcard—they become part of who you are. My family has over 25 years of mountain guiding roots, so from a young age I watched what it meant to respect the land, find quiet peace in nature, and carry a deep curiosity about what lies on the other side of the ridge.

Academically, my path took a very analytical turn. I finished my Bachelor’s in Accounting, which taught me structure and discipline, and now I’m finishing up my Master’s in Data Science. I genuinely enjoy the logic, the problem-solving, and working with complex datasets.

But travel has always been the dream running parallel to everything else. Whenever I get the chance, I pack my backpack and go—scrambling through the boulder fields of Delta Lake, catching the fall colors in Telluride, waking up early in Utah’s red rock country, or getting lost along the coastlines of France and Italy.

Chapter 04

Why I Love Being Out There

01

The Mountains Ground Me

No matter how busy graduate school or work gets, stepping onto a dirt trail or looking up at a massive ridge brings me right back to center. It reminds me how small we are, in the best way possible.

02

Traveling Slow & Simply

I’m not a fan of rushing through 10 tourist spots in a day just to take a picture. I’d rather sit on a rock, watch the light change over a lake, try the local bread, and really feel what a place is like.

03

Curiosity Across Both Worlds

People often ask how accounting, data science, and outdoor adventures connect. To me, they both come down to curiosity—looking closely, finding clarity in chaos, and exploring what’s uncharted.
