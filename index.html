🏔️ 25+ Years Himalayan Legacy

Where Heritage Meets Exploration

Growing up in Nepal, the mountains were more than scenery—they were my family's way of life. For over 25 years, my dad has guided explorers through Nepal’s most demanding and spiritually uplifting trails, from the sacred valleys of Annapurna to the high passes of Everest.

Learning patience, mountain safety, and genuine Himalayan hospitality directly from him inspired me to explore the world. Whether scrambling alpine ridges in the Rocky Mountains and Grand Tetons or wandering the coastlines of the French and Italian Riviera, I bring that same care and deep respect for travel into every itinerary I curate.
